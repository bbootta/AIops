Chapter 01Diagnosis

01

Risk operations shouldn't depend on whoever runs them.

From risk calculation to reporting to validation, risk operations rest on three fragile habits, at 2–4 weeks per model, today (as-is, internal estimate).

Recurring recalculation

Per-analyst scripts recompute the same figures, stress tests, limits, provisions; results are hard to reproduce.

Manual reporting

Risk and validation reports are written from scratch; evidence is collected after the fact.

Individual know-how

Procedures live with people, not systems. When staff move, process disappears.

Chapter 02Architecture

02

Divide the work.
Keep the judgment.

Numbers by the engine. Language by agents. Judgment by people.

Calculation, Deterministic engineENGINE

No LLM in the numbers. Same input, same output, reproducible and verifiable.

Drafting, AI agentsARI · AVI

ARI runs day-to-day risk work: calculations, stress tests, limits, provisions, report drafts. AVI independently monitors and recalculates. Language, never numbers.

Judgment, People + 4-EyesHUMAN

Nothing is final without sign-off by authorized reviewers.

Record, Evidence LedgerLEDGER

Inputs, versions, verdicts, and approvals, logged immutably as work happens.

AI labeling, Agent-drafted content is labeled as AI-generated and remains non-final until approved.
