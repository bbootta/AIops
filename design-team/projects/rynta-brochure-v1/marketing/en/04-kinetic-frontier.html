01 / Why now

Validation shouldn’t depend
on whoever runs it.

Recurring recalculation

Per-analyst scripts recompute the same numbers, results are hard to reproduce.

Manual reporting

Validation reports are written from scratch, evidence is collected after the fact.

Individual know-how

Procedures live with people, not systems, when staff move, process disappears.

2–4 weeksper model, today (as-is, internal estimate)
02 / Our answer

Divide the work.
Keep the judgment.

Calculation Deterministic engine

No LLM in the numbers. Same input, same output, reproducible and verifiable.

Drafting AI agents AVI·ARI

Evidence-linked drafts, gap lists, source collection. Agents write language, never numbers.

Judgment People + 4-Eyes

Nothing is final without sign-off by authorized reviewers.

Record Evidence Ledger

Inputs, versions, verdicts, and approvals, logged immutably as work happens.

AI-generated content notice · Content drafted by the agents (AVI·ARI) is labeled as AI-generated and remains non-final until reviewed and approved by authorized reviewers (4-Eyes).
