01 · DIAGNOSIS

Validation shouldn't depend on whoever runs it.

Model validation today is built on three fragile habits. Each one is solved by a dedicated layer. →

Recurring recalculation

Per-analyst scripts recompute the same numbers every time standards change. Results are hard to reproduce.

Solved by → LAYER 1 · ENGINE

Manual reporting

Validation reports are written from scratch. Evidence is collected after the fact, linked by memory.

Solved by → LAYER 2 · AGENTS

Individual know-how

Procedures live with people, not systems. When staff move, the process disappears with them.

Solved by → LAYER 4 · LEDGER

THE COST TODAY
2–4 weeks per model — today's validation time (as-is, internal estimate)
02 · ARCHITECTURE

Divide the work. Keep the judgment.

1

Calculation — Deterministic engine NO LLM

No LLM in the numbers. Same input, same output.

2

Drafting — AI agents AVI · ARI

Evidence-linked drafts, auto-generated. Agents write language, never numbers.

3

Judgment — People 4-EYES APPROVAL

Nothing is final without sign-off by authorized reviewers.

4

Record — Evidence Ledger IMMUTABLE LOG

Inputs, versions, verdicts, and approvals — logged immutably as work happens.

AI notice — Content drafted by agents (AVI·ARI) is labeled as AI-generated and remains non-final until reviewed and approved by authorized personnel.
