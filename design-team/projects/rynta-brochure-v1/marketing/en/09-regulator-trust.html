Inside — [01 Diagnosis | 02 Architecture | 03 Transition]
01 / DIAGNOSIS

Validation shouldn't depend on whoever runs it.

Model validation today is built on three fragile habits.

Recurring recalculation
Per-analyst scripts recompute the same numbers — results are hard to reproduce.
Manual reporting
Validation reports are written from scratch — evidence is collected after the fact.
Individual know-how
Procedures live with people, not systems. When staff move, process disappears.
2–4 weeks
Per model, today — a bottleneck for audit and review schedules. (As-is, internal estimate)
02 / ARCHITECTURE

Divide the work. Keep the judgment.

Four layers, four responsibilities — each with a clear boundary.

Calculation
Deterministic engine
No LLM in the numbers — same input, same output.
Drafting
AI agents AVI·ARI
Evidence-linked drafts — agents write language, never numbers.
Judgment
People + 4-Eyes approval
Final authority always rests with authorized reviewers.
Record
Evidence Ledger
Logged immutably as work happens.

4-Eyes approval flow

Agent drafts are labeled as AI-generated and remain non-final until two human eyes sign off. Every step is logged in the Evidence Ledger.
