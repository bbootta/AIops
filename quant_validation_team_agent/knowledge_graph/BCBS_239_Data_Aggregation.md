# BCBS 239 Data Aggregation

BCBS 239 is the risk data aggregation and reporting control lens for accuracy,
completeness, timeliness, adaptability, governance, and remediation tracking.

Related nodes: [[Risk_Data_Lineage]], [[Regulatory_Source_Control]],
[[ICAAP]], [[Stress_Testing]], [[Liquidity_Risk_LCR_NSFR]],
[[Korea_Macro_Financial_Data_Map]].

Validation impact:

- Ad-hoc reporting requests require traceable data ownership and transformation
  evidence.
- Weak aggregation controls can override otherwise acceptable quantitative
  results and produce Gray.
- Board or management reporting packs should reconcile to engine outputs.
