# Credit Rating System

A credit rating system combines rating philosophy, grade definitions, obligor or
facility assignment, override governance, performance monitoring, and parameter
linkage.

Related nodes: [[Default_Definition]], [[PD]], [[LGD]], [[EAD]],
[[Calibration]], [[Discriminatory_Power]], [[Override_Governance]],
[[Korean_Supervisory_Rules]].

Validation impact:

- Rating grades must connect to policy-approved default definitions and
  parameter estimation methods.
- Overrides require reason codes, approval evidence, and concentration review.
- Monitoring must distinguish rating performance from PD/LGD/EAD calculation
  output.
