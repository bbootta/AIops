# Domestic Data DART KIND

DART and KIND provide corporate disclosure evidence for issuer, counterparty,
listed-company, event, and governance information.

Related nodes: [[Korea_Macro_Financial_Data_Map]], [[Operational_Risk]],
[[Credit_Rating_System]], [[Stress_Testing]], [[Risk_Data_Lineage]],
[[Regulatory_Source_Control]].

Validation impact:

- Disclosure documents need filing identifiers, filing dates, issuer IDs, and
  extracted-field lineage.
- Disclosure evidence can support judgement context but should not replace
  approved credit or market engine outputs.
- Filing restatements or corrections should trigger data-vintage review.
