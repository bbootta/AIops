# EAD

Exposure at default measures expected exposure when default occurs. Validation
focuses on product mapping, limit history, utilization behavior, CCF evidence,
and backtesting against realized exposure.

Related nodes: [[CCF]], [[Default_Definition]], [[PD]], [[LGD]],
[[Backtesting]], [[Risk_Data_Lineage]], [[Credit_Rating_System]].

Validation impact:

- Revolving and non-revolving products need separate mapping evidence.
- Facility limits and drawdown windows are lineage-critical inputs.
- Missing CCF policy or product-code mapping evidence maps to Gray.
