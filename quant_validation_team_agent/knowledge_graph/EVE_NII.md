# EVE NII

EVE and NII are complementary IRRBB measures. EVE focuses on economic value
sensitivity, while NII focuses on earnings sensitivity over the policy horizon.

Related nodes: [[IRRBB]], [[Stress_Testing]], [[Domestic_Data_KOFIA_Bond]],
[[Domestic_Data_BOK_ECOS]], [[Risk_Data_Lineage]],
[[Regulatory_Source_Control]].

Validation impact:

- Curve version, repricing buckets, behavioral assumptions, and scenario IDs are
  required inputs.
- Different EVE and NII directions should be explained by approved assumptions.
- Missing cash-flow lineage maps to Gray.
