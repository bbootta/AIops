# ICAAP

ICAAP validation checks internal capital adequacy governance, risk-type
aggregation, economic capital or capital planning outputs, stress impacts, risk
appetite linkage, and committee approval.

Related nodes: [[Stress_Testing]], [[BCBS_239_Data_Aggregation]],
[[Operational_Risk]], [[Liquidity_Risk_LCR_NSFR]], [[PD]],
[[Regulatory_Source_Control]].

Validation impact:

- Aggregation logic and diversification treatment need approved policy evidence.
- Reconciliation to finance, risk, and regulatory reports is required.
- Management approval evidence is part of the validation package.
