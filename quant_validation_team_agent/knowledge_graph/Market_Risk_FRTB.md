# Market Risk FRTB

Market risk validation covers trading-book risk-factor eligibility, standardised
and model-based approaches, desk-level governance, backtesting, P&L attribution,
and market-data source integrity.

Related nodes: [[VaR_Backtesting]], [[P_L_Attribution]],
[[Regulatory_Source_Control]], [[Risk_Data_Lineage]], [[Basel_Framework]],
[[Domestic_Data_KRX]].

Validation impact:

- Market data source, price validation, and position snapshot version are
  mandatory evidence.
- Desk-level model results should link to approved scope and limits.
- Missing source validation creates Gray even when VaR figures are present.
