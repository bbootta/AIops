# P L Attribution

P&L attribution links front-office risk-theoretical P&L, actual or hypothetical
P&L, and model risk-factor coverage.

Related nodes: [[Market_Risk_FRTB]], [[VaR_Backtesting]],
[[Risk_Data_Lineage]], [[Domestic_Data_KRX]], [[Regulatory_Source_Control]].

Validation impact:

- P&L source, valuation adjustments, and unexplained residuals must be evidenced.
- Attribution failure is a governance issue, not just a number.
- Data-source mismatch between front office and risk engines requires Action
  Notice.
