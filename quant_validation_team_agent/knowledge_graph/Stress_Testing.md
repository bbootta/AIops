# Stress Testing

Stress testing links scenarios, macro variables, risk-factor paths, engine
outputs, management actions, capital and liquidity impact, and governance
review.

Related nodes: [[Downturn_Adjustment]], [[IRRBB]], [[Liquidity_Risk_LCR_NSFR]],

- Macro paths should trace to domestic or official scenario data sources.
- Cross-risk packs with unclear ownership map to `multi_risk_or_unclear` and
  Gray.
